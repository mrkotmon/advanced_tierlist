Three steps, about a minute

Boards start empty and stay editable. Nothing is locked in until you decide it is.

STEP 01

Add your items

Paste a list, upload images, or pick from a template. Movies, frameworks, pizza toppings — whatever needs settling.

STEP 02

Drag them into tiers

Six rows by default, renameable and recolorable. Move an item as many times as you need to.

STEP 03

Send the link

Anyone with the link joins instantly. Their changes appear on your screen as they make them.
